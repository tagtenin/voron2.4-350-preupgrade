# Safety notes

- The current hotend limit remains 270 C. Verify the exact thermistor and heater ratings before raising it.
- Carbon-filled materials require an abrasion-resistant nozzle.
- Do not trust Survey Touch until repeatability is verified with a clean nozzle and rigid toolhead.
- Verify the expanded bed mesh area is mechanically reachable before running an unattended mesh.
- Re-measure input shaping before enabling it; the old values were intentionally removed.
